# Game Master's Notes

### The Church of the Abiding Vigilante

This is not your average sect of Abadar. The vigilante known to Diddi as "Fixer" was one of the original gladiators who came to Diddi to investigate a fight-fixing scheme. As a free gladiator (not slave), he had been approached by shadowy individuals to take a dive in the ring or else. He understood also that some of his fights had begun to be too easy, and his payouts were becoming less and less as his odds got better. The group that was betting on him to win (because his opponents were diving) were now planning to bet against him while the odds of him winning were high, expecting a huge payday.

Diddi and Fixer found the gambling syndicate, and he enlisted honest free gladiators to take care of them, Kaer Maga style. The unfortunate side effect was that the gladiatorial ring was heavily subsidized by the syndicate, and folded. They owned many of the slaves that fought in the ring, and those slaves were liquidated to pay the syndicate's debts. That left a number of free gladiators without a gig.

Another of the gladiators, Maxon "Knuckles" Arvane, a former priest of Abadar, gathered the remainder of the gladiators and proposed a new vocation: street justice in the name of Abadar. The streets are a lawless place but there are pockets of citizens who, despite their evil, monstrous nature, are looking for outlets for their various appetites. They also just want their street to be quiet.

Knuckles proposed that the gladiators become civic organizers for local justice. Not to police the streets day and night, but to respond to crime and disturbances if nobody else does, by recruiting the less savory characters who have the appetite for a beat-down... or a meal.

Fixer was assigned Diddi's neighborhood in Oriat.

The Abiding Vigilante characters should be built as equal parts gladiator, game masters, and Abadarian inquisitors. They tend to be very good at diplomacy, sensing motives, investigation, local cultural knowledge, chasing, and running games of all kinds. To that end, they are trained in devising games that provide the parties involved with no advantage due to their race, profession, or life status (living or undead). Two grandmothers with equal strength might be encouraged to settle their differences with a public fist-fight. An orc and a gnome might settle their differences with guessing the weight of a barrel of ale. Two vampires might get into a public drinking game. Each time, the Abiding Vigilantes make it a public event, and betting is thoroughly encouraged, but the Vigilantes require that they do not take sides. They do, however, take their 10% rake of the betting pool to fund their activies.

If there's a crime where their help is requested, they organize posses. These look like wild hunts through the streets, with a myriad of various races and creatures, depending on the neighborhood. You might one night see a vampire, a werewolf, three pixies, and a horde of intelligent giant ants chasing down a murder suspect, all led by an Abiding Vigilante. Free of charge.

The Church of the Abiding Vigilante members are welcome in inns across Kaer Maga, and usually never have to pay for a meal, a bath, or a common service. There is one district where they are not welcome, which is Bis. There, a single Vigilante is responsible for coordinating with the local golem police force who execute justice in their own way.

Fixer's list of equipment should include a heavy crossbow with bolt case, two cesti (for when things get personal), leather lamellar armor, and a backpack containing numerous tools for problem resolution (peaceful and not-so-peaceful): wrapped beat-down sticks, punching gloves, chess board, decks of cards, dice, hot sauce dispensers for heat-eating contests, etc.

Note that the Bank of Abadar offers no standing whatsoever to the Church of the Abiding Vigilante. They see the entire organization as a cult of heretics. Until they are needed to resolve a dispute or hunt down a thief, in which case they are quietly called.

### Sufferer's Digest

This publication is meant as a wholesome family periodical in the style of "Reader's Digest" with the moral assumptions of Zon-Kuthon.

Deadline "uploads" stories to his editor in Nidal via a shared pair of journals. Anything written in one automatically is seen in the other. His editor is not manic, or even particularly driven by screams, but the uniqueness and quality of the suffering. In his view, he is trying to expand the suffering horizons of his clientele in intellectual and thought-provoking ways, regardless of whether blood is involved or not.

Sufferer's Digest has a competitor, _The Daily Flogger_, that is the opposite. It is all about screams and cheap suffering thrills. It thrives on big headlines and promises of pain and blood. Their editor is a task-master, forcing his journalists to meet unrealistic timelines and forcing them to seek out the biggest battles and the most dangerous beats... often to their detriment. _The Daily Flogger_ has a recruitment problem, so the editor is always trying to get Deadline to join.

Here's an example article written by Deadline, one of Sufferer's Digest premier beat-writers, about a week spent following Mop around and writing about jump-scares.

***

The Delight of Social Isolation Brought About By Addiction to Jump Scares

Location: Kaer Maga, Oriat District

Subject: Mop, the Quasit

I was first introduced to this curious little creature of the Abyss while frequenting the shop of a local diviner by the name of Diddi. You've seen other articles of mine considering the loss of feeling in her stomach due to repeated lacerations in the area.

This time, I learned that Mop may have a peculiar addiction to striking fear into the hearts of others. This seemed truly fascinating.

I followed him throughout the streets of Oriat, thinking myself unseen. While there was enjoyment in the act of jump-scaring the local townsfolk, his expression returned to a frown the moment the jump-scare passed. He seemed to notice that others recoiled at his presence. He often decided to travel invisibly, so as to make his isolation seem self-imposed. Of course, while invisible, it was impossible to determine what his expression was, but I heard no giggling or any other indication of delight in the interim.

As always, I look to provide ways for the discerning reader to join in the suffering of my subjects.

Reproducing this addiction at home will require some creativity. You must have at your disposal the means to produce a variety of jump-scares. You should invest in some orisons if you haven't already. Voices of dark fey work well as an appetizer. If you are not magically inclined, your next best option is the purchase of several varied traps from your local trapsmith. Never underestimate the terror that can be achieved by lunging at someone's throat with your bare hands. And consider carrying around a great axe, just to see the looks it garners when you draw it suddenly for no apparent reason.

Next, identify your audience. You should spread your jump-scares around. Do not keep attacking the same target, because of the psychology of diminished returns. Slowly, over the course of a few days, you'll find the scare is equally exhilarating to you as it is to the target. But, you'll find that your social standing will start dropping. Friends will stop visiting. Neighbors will pass you on the street nervously. This is the effect you are after, ladies and gentlemen.

Of course, as with all addictions (as you may remember my last article regarding the addiction to swallowing razor blades), the hardest part of stopping an addiction is realizing you have one. Can you stop thinking about tomorrow's scares when you go to bed? Are you planning your route home from work to include dark alleys? Do you smile anytime your neighbor looks over their shoulder?

These are signs that you might be arriving at addiction. It's imperative that you not stop now, while your addiction is in its formative stages. This is the time to truly spice it up, keep it fresh, and keep smiling while that haunting feeling eats you up inside.

Imagine the glorious suffering you are spreading to others with your jump-scares. Nothing brings delight to the Midnight Lord more than terror delivered in a dark alley. Please milk it for what it's worth. Enjoy the tension in the air between you and those who have heard of your newfound hobby. Relish the complete lack of visitors to your door.

Contemplate the idea of taking your jump-scares to other neighborhoods. Indulge in scaring groups at a time. Only then will you truly enjoy this particular form of madness.

Once you are done, burn the evidence. Attempt a recovery. Notice the difficulty of being genial to your neighbor once more. Endure the pain of winning back their trust, apologizing for attempting to strangle them... in their sleep.

Occasionally, perhaps once a decade, schedule a relapse. Recovering from relapses is a good distraction from the routine. Have your spouse engage in a relapse with you. Co-dependence with a shared addiction truly spices up the bedroom and makes the experience much more difficult.

Until next time, this is Deadline, your favorite tormentor. May all your torments be shared with loved ones.

## Prologue: Two Mouths, One Shadow

The children sat in a ragged circle in the tall grass of the park, their eyes fixed on two black shadows perched on an ancient, iron-grey limb. The ravens were waiting for stragglers to move in, and the whispers to get quieter.

"A story for the young-lings, to feed their hungry minds!" Hugrinn croaks, his beak sharp as a needle.

"Which story? There are so many to recount." Muninn added, the words clicking into place.

A broad grin stretches Hugrinn's face... "Beowulf! A both sad and lovely tale..."

Muninn waves a wing... "No! They'll never remember it. Too boring for young minds."

Hugrinn replies, "How about the saga of Sigurd the Dragon Slayer?"

Muninn moans, "I told that story last week, remember? Pick again!"

Hugrinn is thoughtful, then chirps: "A tale not told in a long while. The Edge-Kin Blades!"

Muninn nods and begins... "Listen well children. Commit this to mind and memory, for it is a tale of ..."

Hugrinn interrupts... "Cah! I'm the heart, the song, AND the mind... who better to tell the tale than me, for the blades are... well let's just say I can tell the story better than you. I connect all the pieces into the epic tale that it is. It's... mine!" Hugrinn beats his wings and repeats, "mine! mine! mine!".

Muninn, rolling his obsidian eyes, shakes his head... a slow shake practiced from seemingly the beginning of time itself. "You... ALWAYS... want to own the tale. Thankfully, I'm here... to keep the oath of the thing true. Proceed."

Hugrinn continues, cackling clear his throat. The children stared curiously but intently, mesmerized by the movements and cackling of the ravens.

"It all started at the end of the Seige of Leicester, 941 Anno Dommini, in eastern England. I believe it was... the rains of late spring.".

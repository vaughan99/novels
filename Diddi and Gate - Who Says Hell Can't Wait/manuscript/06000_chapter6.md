# Chapter Six: The Devil That You Know... Now

So once Quill heard "tomorrow", he instantly started praying. Burning incense, chanting, the whole dog and pony show.

And Gate appeared once again. This time the prayer went straight to the top. No mucking around with auctions.

Rezzen explained that the Openers had both pieces. The Closers were defeated. They put the two pieces together, the language suddenly became readable and... And all the Openers had to do was wait until some star was in line with another star, or some such. Put it simply, wait until "tomorrow at dawn".

That jibed with the timeline of when Gate, and all the other infernal witnesses, were to _voop_ into Kaer Maga. Lake's edge in the Hospice District. Right where you're sitting now in fact.

That's when Rezzen laid a doozy on us. You see, he wasn't going to let the big "A" or any of the other devils have all the fun ruining Geryon's day. He had prayed to Magrim. And Magrim talked to every dwarven deity in the pantheon. Except Droskar, well because Droskar is Droskar.

They were going to be there too.

But there was more.

You see, Abadar, the God of Cities and Walls and Vaults and anything that makes a city get along nicely (well except maybe the taxation part), he has a vested interest in this city too. And Magrim contacted him. And he decided he wanted to show up. You know, to make sure innocent civilians don't die.

After he heard all this, Gate waved his hand, and Rezzen's bracers dissolved, and Wings re-appeared. She dusted herself off, gave Rezzen a nasty stare, and joined Gate by his side. She swore she'd _never_ be enchanted into another object ever again. But I could see the smile in the corner of her mouth. Secretly, I think she enjoyed learning a whole new fighting style.

Rezzen was stoic. I think he just wanted his part in this to end, so he could go back to Magrim's cloister. Did you know he founded the Guardians of the Last Citadel monastery? Right here in Kaer Maga. He's a minor celebrity now.

Wings didn't stick around. After Gate whispered to her, she vooped out. He must have given her an assignment.

So, the next morning, here's how it went down.

The city was still clueless. People were waking up like it was a normal day. Markets were starting to open.

Suddenly, Geryon _voops_ in with ten pit-fiend bodyguards, and a host of warrior devils. They established a perimeter. Right here.

Frightened citizens were there. They stood motionless, in total fear. Others looked out through their windows at the scene. They gathered their families, their servants. They thought they might be witnessing something truly devastating about to happen.

Then Geryon started giving a speech. The lake started to drain away just by the shoreline. A hole in the lake was opening, driven by some pretty weird magic.

Now, I didn't want to miss this. I stood over by the Temple of Calistria with Mop, Quill, Rezzen, Deadline and Shoes. I figured if things went sideways, the Calistrians are pretty handy in a street-fight. Rezzen I knew could handle himself.

Anyway, Geryon goes on and on about how Golarion is doomed. Big smile on his snake-face. Until...

Voop. Voop. Voop-voop-voop-voop-voop.

Devils started appearing all around him. Silk-laden representatives of various arch-devils. Geryon seemed pleased. He wanted all the arch-devils to know what he did.

So he continued his speech.

Then the big "A" vooped in, with _his_ entourage. Geryon smiled even broader. He wanted the big "A" to personally see what he had done.

He cast the final spell. A shaft of light came forth from the whirlpool, the hole down into the Underdark was complete. The Seal opened. Lots of lightning, and steam, and... nothing else.

The big "A" stood there and clapped. Slowly. Mockingly.

Geryon's smile faded.

Then... voop. Voop. Voop-voop-voop-voop-voop.

Dwarven gods and _their_ entourages started appearing. Massive dwarven constructs. Ancient dwarven warrior-ghosts fully decked out in adamantine platemail. The big "A" took a step back. He literally took a step back.

Then another major voop. Abadar and his host of angels. Mithral crossbows pointed in every direction. A big smile on his face.

Abadar's angels started collecting civilians. Having them stand behind the angels. One tried to step in front of me, but I told it that it was ruining my view.

Anyway, the beach was _way_ overcrowded with firepower.

Geryon's bodyguards looked at each other. They shrugged. They stepped aside.

Now, the big "A" had his bodyguards step forward. Geryon tried to voop away, but something prevented him. Not sure what. Probably the biggest kind of devil-magic.

The big "A" gave the signal, and his pit-fiends drove their adamantine lances into Geryon, pinning him to the beach. But he didn't die. You don't kill a ninety-foot snake archdevil with a dozen spears.

That's when the big "A" stepped forward, took his giant mace, and smacked Geryon upside the head. Geryon was so dazed, he couldn't even defend himself. Geryon was beaten over and over. Everybody else just watched.

Then, there was one last voop.

It was Gate. And he had a stack of infernal parchment.

The big "A" turned, walked ten steps back behind his bodyguards, and prepared to give a grand speech just as Geryon gave his last breath.

But, Gate stepped forward. He took his own lance, and with Geryon's final breath, pinned a piece of parchment to his body.

The big "A" had just finished clearing his throat. He cast a curled brow at Gate. "What... are you doing?"

Gate turned to face the big "A". He said, "Claiming."

The big "A" narrowed his eyes at Gate, and lifted his hand. His bodyguards readied their claws.

Gate pulled forth a copy of the parchment. Several copies actually, and started handing them out. As he did so, he started quoting,

“I, the devil commonly known as Gate, my true name being withheld pursuant to infernal custom and protections against unauthorized invocation, hereby lay claim to the presently unclaimed asset consisting of the mortal remains, appurtenances, attached portals, fixtures, contents, and derivative interests of Geryon, formerly of Stygia.
“Said asset presently rests upon the Prime Material Plane, in the city of Kaer Maga, Hospice District, outside the territorial possession of Stygia, and is, at the moment of filing, unencumbered by any prior perfected claim.
“Therefore, pursuant to the Doctrine of First Possession, commonly rendered ‘Finders Keepers,’ under First Premises, Article One, Title Two, Subsection Three, I hereby perfect possession on behalf of Mammon.”

He looked the big "A" right in the eye. “I intend to make it real estate.”

The big "A" stood there, lien in hand, his left eye twitching.

There arose a round of applause. From the dwarves. The angels. The townsfolk. Even a few of the big "A"'s own people.

But the big "A" merely turned, his giant black cloak swinging the air. Voop. He left. The manuscript fell from his hands, and landed harmlessly on the ground.

I looked over at Deadline. I'd never seen his hands move so fast. He was writing, drawing, measuring. I'd never seen him so happy. He ignored the gods, devils, angels, everything. He walked right up to Geryon and started noting every detail.

One by one, each of the devils, angels, gods, warriors and constructs all vooped away. Leaving Gate standing, hands on hips, staring at Geryon's corpse. His corpse was turning to stone at that point.

Also, the whirlpool collapsed on itself. I have to believe that water rushed into the room behind the Seal. Whatever was behind the Seal is now under a mile of water. Good luck to anyone who wants to do an archaelogical dig.

Wings vooped in, turned to Gate and smiled. Apparently she had been sent back to Mammon's palace to see what the initial reactions were. All positive.

Gate turned and approached me. "Well, you mentioned your tent had thin walls. How would you like to relocate? Your divination skills are quite... _profitable_."

I told him I'd think about it.

I wasn't sure I wanted a business partner. Or a landlord. But having both really put my mind at ease. I mean, having Mammon and one of his upwardly mobile cohorts personally invested in your continued survival does have its advantages. And now I really don't have to worry about thieves.

So they finally got a yes out of me.

And we struck a deal. I'd move my business here, in the remains of Geryon's stone corpse. And he would keep everything I made in his vault. Which I think was Geryon's spleen. Maybe liver. Not sure.

But it means he has to keep the common iron too. And anything else I get as payments. It's funny to think there's a coop full of chickens being guarded as closely as a stack of platinum bars. But hey, this is Kaer Maga. Stranger things happen here.

Didn't take long for Gate to open doorways into Geryon's body. He's found a world of goodies hidden in there. Can't go into it all, but let's say there's doorways to things you can only dream of. Now his transition to pit-fiend is probably a matter of months.

Once you get stronger, Gate might even have some work for you. Not every secret Geryon owned wants to... co-operate... with asset ownership transition. You get the picture.

Oh, and don't try to go down. It's guarded by the largest mimic you ever saw. Let's call him "Guard Dog". Only he's a chest. He was a wand. Didn't I tell you that story? I think I did. Anyway, the two hedge wizards didn't want it, and it needed a home. Quill filed it under "escrow" and, well, now it's a member of the family. He's a good "Guard Dog". Yes he is.

Once I finally moved in, Gate had a present for Quill. Now, you must know, Mammon and his devils _never_ give presents. Unless they have a purpose.

So Gate presented Quill a silken wrapping with a bow-tie. Quill took it in his hands, shivering to find out what Gate had gotten him.

It was a copy of _First Premises_, but not just any copy. It was an original first copy of the 64th rewrite, signed by the author: Ythakisdurpenombstat the Uncombobulated. There was even a bookmark that led to the exact line of Finders Keepers.

Gate smiled. He told Quill that if Quill had thought of it, he could have invoked Finders Keepers himself, and own the entire thing. He would have been the richest imp in all of the history of imp-dom.

"I hate you."

Now Quill, don't take it personally. We all miss out on great opportunities. You know how disappointed I was to have all those gods literally a few steps away, and have them all voop away before I could introduce myself. I _still_ haven't met a god. Very disappointing.

Well, that's what happens when you have no stature. You see, the other Augurs ate high-status nobles. Torntusk? He ate the famous war general. Greenhalo? She ate the high priestess. Me? I ate the chambermaid. I guess that's why Blood-Drop started having visions about dirty bedsheets when he mainlined my blood.

But I have no regrets. Chambermaids know all the little secrets. And those secrets are the ones that solve big mysteries.

Deadline took a break from _Sufferer's Digest_ and went solo. He got a major award for his five-part book series: "The Death of Geryon, A Profile In Epic Embarrassment". Once we get a copy, I'll let you read it.

So now, give your name to Quill, and your occupation. Then tell me what you brought.

And don't worry, Geryon won't bite you. He just likes to... stare.

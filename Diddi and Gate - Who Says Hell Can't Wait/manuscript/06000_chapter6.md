# Chapter Six: The Devil That You Know... Now

So once Quill heard "tomorrow", he instantly started praying. Burning incense, chanting, the whole dog and pony show.

And Gate vooped in once again. This time the prayer went straight to the top. No mucking around with auctions.

Rezzen was bloody and bruised. Mop looked at him like he was a prize hog at a farm expo. He explained that the battle was a failure, which means success for those of you keeping track. Rezzen and Wings were defeated, but just barely.

So, the vault could only be opened at particular windows of time in the year, and the next one was "tomorrow at dawn".

That jibed with the timeline Gate had just been given by Mammon. Tomorrow at dawn, all the archdevil's representatives were to voop into Kaer Maga and watch a grand spectacle. Right where you're sitting now in fact.

That's when Rezzen laid a doozy on us. You see, he wasn't going to let the big "A" or any of the other devils have all the fun ruining Geryon's day. He had prayed to Magrim. And Magrim talked to every dwarven deity in the pantheon. Except Droskar, well because Droskar is Droskar.

They were going to be there too.

But there was more.

You see, Abadar, the God of Cities and Walls and Vaults and anything that makes a city get along nicely (well except maybe the taxation part), he has a vested interest in this city too. And Magrim contacted him. And he decided he wanted to show up. You know, to make sure innocent civilians don't die.

After he heard all this, Gate waved his hand, and Rezzen's bracers dissolved, and Wings re-appeared. She dusted herself off, gave Rezzen a nasty stare, and joined Gate by his side. She swore she'd _never_ be enchanted into another object ever again. But I could see the smile in the corner of her mouth. Secretly, I think she enjoyed learning a whole new fighting style.

Rezzen was stoic. I think he just wanted his part in this to end, so he could go back to Magrim's cloister. Did you know he founded the Guardians of the Last Citadel monastery? Right here in Kaer Maga. He's a minor celebrity now. But I digress.

Wings didn't stick around. After Gate whispered to her, she vooped out. He must have given her an assignment.

So, the next morning, here's how it went down.

The city was still clueless. People were waking up like it was a normal day. Markets were starting to open.

Suddenly, Geryon _voops_ in with ten pit-fiend bodyguards, and a host of warrior devils. They established a perimeter. Right here in Oriat.

Frightened citizens were there. They stood motionless, in total fear. Others looked out through their windows at the scene. They gathered their families, their servants. They thought they might be witnessing something truly devastating about to happen.

Voop. Voop. Voop-voop-voop-voop-voop.

Devils started appearing all around him. Silk-laden representatives of various arch-devils. Geryon seemed pleased. He wanted all the arch-devils to know what he did.

Now, I didn't want to miss this. I stood over by the Street of Sighs with Mop, Quill, Rezzen, Deadline and Shoes. I figured if things went sideways, the Sweettalkers can't scream... so at least my death would be a little quieter.

Geryon and his pit fiends smashed a few buildings to make sure the crowds had a clearer view of him. His face could not contain his pleasure.

"BEHOLD, KAER MAGA! YOUR DOOM IS AT HAND! For five millennia, sleeping beneath you, a horror has been brewing. From the last days of the ancient Caulborn kingdom, the Brothers of the Seal have been hiding the _true_ destiny of all Golarion. Eethva. Bothufus. Yackbaaaaa!"

A terrible earthquake erupted, and a fissure ten feet in diameter opened in the ground at the entrance to the Undercity.

Then the big "A" vooped in, with _his_ entourage. Geryon smiled even broader. He wanted the big "A" to personally see what he had done.

"Behold! As I reach down into the Underdark! Past the Brothers and their citadels. Past the caverns and vaults of the Caulborn, to the very Seal itself. Icksa. Returna. Ohssaaaaan!"

The fissure widened. Lightning started to erupt from the edges. People held each other in fear. Quill was documenting everything. I think Mop was snacking on a rat, his eyes fixed on the terror like he was at the puppet theater.

"Woe to those whose souls are unprepared! I, the Mighty, Wise, and Forgiving Geryon, shall accept any soul that wishes to be spared! Start your prayer now, oh little mortals, for you have but moments for me to hear your cries! Yield to me, Kaer Maga, and I shall spare you! Zeelan. Foruus. Yickbaaaaah!"

A shaft of light emanated from inside the fissure, followed by crackles of lightning at the edges. The sky was turning black, as the mist had formed dark clouds that were swirling like a slow tornado.

Geryon turned and slithered along the fissure's edge. As he spoke, he turned back and forth, slithering his body to encompass the small fissure.

"Now witness, Kaer Maga! I, the Lord of Secrets, the Archdevil of Ruin, shall expose the secret of the Caulborn. They have been hiding an ancient hunger. A being of such immense hate, it could only have come from the Great Beyond! A being whose name itself has been lost to history, but whose mere existence SHALL SHAKE YOU TO... YOUR... BONES! BEHOLD! THE GREAT OLD ONE!!!"

Geryon cast his arms wide. He turned his head. The vault beneath the Seal opened.

Lots of lightning, and steam, and... nothing else.

Geryon reset his arms. Cast them wide again. He kept his smile. But still. Nothing.

The big "A" stood there and clapped. Slowly. Mockingly.

Geryon's smile faded.

Then the big "A" spoke.

"Geryon, when you're done with... whatever spells you _think_ you're casting... be a good little devil and summon that goat's head dish with radishes and yozanka weed. You'll find the recipe inside the vault. It's called Fezzark. Oh, and I like my goat's head raw... like Baphomet's-head-on-a-platter raw."

Geryon froze. The smile turned to horror.

Then... voop. Voop. Voop-voop-voop-voop-voop.

Dwarven gods and _their_ entourages started appearing. Massive dwarven constructs. Ancient dwarven warrior-ghosts fully decked out in adamantine platemail. The big "A" took a step back. He literally took a step back.

Then another major voop. Abadar and his host of angels. Mithral crossbows pointed in every direction. A big smile on his face.

Abadar's angels started collecting civilians. Having them stand behind the angels. One tried to step in front of me, but I told it that it was ruining my view.

Anyway, Oriat was _way_ overcrowded with firepower.

Geryon's bodyguards looked at each other. They shrugged. They stepped aside.

Now, the big "A" had his bodyguards step forward. Geryon tried to voop away, but something prevented him. Not sure what. Probably the biggest kind of devil-magic.

The big "A" gave the signal, and his pit-fiends drove their adamantine lances into Geryon, pinning him to the ground. But he didn't die. You don't kill a ninety-foot snake archdevil with a dozen spears.

That's when the big "A" stepped forward, took his giant mace, and smacked Geryon upside the head. Geryon was so dazed, he couldn't even defend himself. Geryon was beaten over and over. Everybody else just watched.

Then, there was one last voop.

It was Gate. And he had a stack of infernal parchment under one arm, a sack of platinum coins in the other, and a small sand-glass tied to his neck.

The big "A" turned, walked ten steps back behind his bodyguards, and prepared to give a grand speech just as Geryon gave his last breath.

But, Gate stepped forward. He took one of the lances, freed it, took a piece of parchment, and just after Geryon's final breath, pinned the parchment to his body. He took out a small book and a piece of charcoal, and wrote a mark.

Gate then turned. "Excuse me!"

The big "A" had just finished clearing his throat. He cast a curled brow at Gate. "What... are you doing?"

Gate turned to face the big "A". He said, "Ummm... Claiming."

The big "A" narrowed his eyes at Gate, and lifted his hand. His bodyguards readied their claws.

Gate pulled forth a copy of the parchment. Several copies actually, and started handing them out. As he did so, he started quoting,

“I, the devil commonly known as Gate, my true name being withheld pursuant to infernal custom and protections against unauthorized invocation, hereby lay claim to the presently unclaimed asset consisting of the mortal remains, materials, fluids, organs, attached portals, fixtures, contents, and derivative interests of Geryon, formerly of Stygia.
“Said asset presently rests upon the world of Golarion, in the city of Kaer Maga, Oriat District, outside the territorial possession of Stygia, and is, at the moment of filing, unencumbered by any prior perfected claim. Geryon, having originated as an asura rana rather than a devil, possesses no posthumous claim to infernal remains under the protections afforded to native devils.
“Therefore, pursuant to the Doctrine of First Possession, commonly rendered ‘Finders Keepers,’ under First Premises, Article One, Title Two, Subsection Three, I hereby perfect possession on behalf of Mammon. All subsequent claims thereto shall be deemed null and void, without recourse or appeal. All rights, titles, interests, and privileges of possession are hereby reserved exclusively to Mammon, his agents, assigns, and successors in perpetuity.
"In furtherance of this claim, Mammon hereby assumes the outstanding property liabilities arising from Geryon's destruction of the surrounding structures and improvements, and shall discharge those liabilities pursuant to the established Kaer Magan custom of 'You-Break-It, You-Buy-It.' Such reimbursement shall constitute consideration for the transfer of the affected properties into Mammon's possession, with compensation payable to surviving owners or their lawful heirs. The Caulborn vault, Seal, and enclosing structure at the bottom of this shaft, having no tenants, agents, or surviving owners, shall be included in this claim, and form the boundary of the claim."

He drew a deep breath, then continued. He took another look at the book, waited another breath's worth of time, and spoke.

"The paperwork has already been filed in Dis... just... NOW. This notification is merely a... courtesy."

He snapped the book closed and looked the big "A" right in the eye with a big smile. “I intend to open Kaer Maga's first Bank of Mammon.”

The big "A" stood there, parchment in hand, his left eye twitching.

There arose a round of applause. First from the townsfolk. Then from the dwarves. The angels, even a few of the big "A"'s own people started clapping.

But the big "A" merely turned, his giant black cloak swinging in the air. Voop. He left. The parchment fell from his hands, and landed harmlessly on the ground where it burned into a poof of ash.

I looked over at Deadline. He was searching his pack for his journal, and for the first time in his life, he couldn't find it. That grown man cried. At least for a moment.

So, one by one, each of the devils, angels, gods, warriors and constructs all vooped away. Leaving Gate talking to survivors and heirs, marking property lines, and occasionally stopping to stare at Geryon's corpse. His corpse was turning to stone at that point, and the fissure that he opened was in the center of his coils.

Wings vooped in, turned to Gate and smiled. She had a book in her hands as well.

I had never seen her smile until that moment. Then she handed Gate a piece of stamped parchment. I don't read Infernal, but I recognize an APPROVED stamp when I see one.

Apparently she had been sent to the Fallen Fastness in Dis... that's where the Infernal Court has its Property and Claims Division. She had been sent the previous night to wait in its queue with the paperwork. She saw an Asmodean in the queue just behind her, hiding his paperwork. She said she filed just before he did.

The two books in the Gate and Wings' hands also looked _very_ familiar. I nudged Deadline to take a look, and lo and behold, Wings had stolen the two journals... one from Deadline, and one from his editor. Wings and Gate had used them to coordinate the filing.

Deadline marched right up to Wings and held out his hand. Wings smiled, politely said "thank you", and handed him his journal. She took the other journal from Gate, and vooped away. Deadline got confirmation a few minutes later that his editor had gotten his back too.

Gate didn't miss a beat. He turned to the crowd. "All affected property owners or their heirs please step forward for compensation. Please have your deeds or other proof of ownership in hand. Thank you."

After he made all the contacts he needed to secure the purchase of the ruins Gate turned and approached me. "Well, you mentioned your tent had thin walls. How would you like to relocate? Your divination skills are quite... _profitable_. And I can't envision a more secure place. I'm moving here myself. There's a lot of work to make that fissure and the Seal's vault into one super-vault. I could use a partner, and a tenant while I do the build-out. I can put you right... there, under the archway made by that coil."

I told him I'd think about it.

I wasn't sure I wanted a business partner. Or a landlord. But having both really put my mind at ease. I mean, having Mammon and a few of his upwardly mobile cohorts personally invested in your continued survival does have its advantages. And now I really don't have to worry about thieves.

So they finally got a yes out of me.

And we struck a deal. I'd move my business here, in the remains of Geryon's stone corpse. And he would keep everything I made in his vault. The vault that starts with Geryon's corpse and leads a mile down into the Underdark, culminating in the Seal's hidden vault. I think the entrance _was_ Geryon's spleen. Maybe liver. Not sure.

But it means he has to keep the common iron too. And anything else I get as payments. It's funny to think there's a coop full of chickens being guarded as closely as a stack of platinum bars. But hey, this is Kaer Maga. Stranger things happen here.

Didn't take long for Gate to open doorways in Geryon's brain. He's found a world of goodies hidden in there. Can't go into it all, but let's say there's doorways to things you can only dream of. Now Gate's promotion to pit-fiend is probably just a formality.

Oh, and don't try to go down. The entrance is guarded by the largest mimic you ever saw. Let's call him "Guard Dog". He knows the smell of us here at the shop, but that's it. 

Didn't I tell you that story? About the mimic child. I think I did. Anyway, the two hedge wizards didn't want it, and it needed a home. Quill filed it under "escrow" and, well, it ate the escrow chest. And it _became_ the escrow chest. He's a good "Guard Dog". _Yes he is_. Now we just need Mop to stop feeding him.

By the way, if you go looking for the hundred-tooth mithral key, you'll need to coax it out of Guard Dog. It's somewhere down in his gullet. Good luck with that.

Once we finally moved in, Gate had a present for Quill. Now, you must know, Mammon and his devils _never_ give presents. Unless they have a purpose.

So Gate presented Quill a silken wrapping with a bow-tie. Quill took it in his hands, shivering to find out what Gate had gotten him.

It was a copy of _First Premises_, but not just any copy. It was an original first copy of the 64th rewrite, signed by the author: Ythakisdurpenombstat the Uncombobulated. There was even a bookmark that led to the exact line of Finders Keepers.

Gate smiled. He told Quill that if Quill had thought of it, he could have invoked Finders Keepers himself, and own the entire thing. He would have been the richest imp in all of the history of imp-dom.

"I hate you."

Now Quill, don't take it personally. We all miss out on great opportunities. 

"Heehee. Fat imp could have been fattest imp of all!"

Mop, I told you to be quiet.

"Sorry Miss Diddi."

You know how disappointed I was to have all those gods literally a few steps away, and have them all voop away before I could introduce myself? I _still_ haven't met a god. Very disappointing.

Well, that's what happens when you have no status.

That reminds me. The other _true_ Augurs got their powers from eating high-status nobles. Torntusk? He ate the famous Caulborn war general. Greenhalo? She ate the Caulborn high priestess. Me? Hahaha. I ate the Caulborn Queen's chambermaid. I guess that's why Blood-Drop started having visions about dirty bedsheets when he mainlined my blood.

But I have no regrets. Chambermaids know all the little secrets. And those secrets are the ones that solve big mysteries.

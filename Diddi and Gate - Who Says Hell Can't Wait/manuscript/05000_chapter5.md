# Chapter Five: The Devil You Don't Know

Now Gate's presence in my little tent was certainly shocking, but not _that_ shocking. He _vooped_ in and announced himself. Apparently devils do that occasionally.

Quill introduced himself. I think he was shaking in awe. He may have even peed a little.

Now Mop, don't start making fun of Quill. You'd pee yourself too if Socothbenoth suddenly showed up to give you the spanking you deserve. Go sit in the corner until my story's done.

Now, where was I?

Oh yes. Rezzen was there. Now Droskar and Mammon actually have a lot in common. They're willing to do just about anything to get a gold piece. But, Mammon wouldn't hesitate to throw anyone under a cart wheel, including his own mother, to get that gold piece. I'm pretty sure in Mammon's mortal life he literally did that.

So Gate and Rezzen met. And Gate told him what needed to be done.

Help the Openers. Open the Seal.

Pick your jaws up off the ground. You heard me right.

You see, Gate did a little digging. He has an erinyes devil sidekick... let's call her Wings. She's really good at infiltration, kicking down doors, and kicking a little ass when needed. She's Gate's right-hand she-devil.

She learned that Geryon was quietly pulling together his private bodyguards for a big move. Don't ask me how she figured that out. She must have connections. She might be part succubus. We're looking into that.

So, not only was Geryon planning to open the Seal, but it looked like he was going to be present when he did.

To claim credit for it. To gloat.

That's when Mammon received a request. To send a representative to Kaer Maga. To witness a grand event. The culmination of Geryon's power.

Mammon knew just who he would send. Gate.

Now, what was _really_ interesting is that Mammon also knew that the big "A" didn't try to put a stop to it. Whatever it was.

Ordinarily, if the big "A" was about to be one-upped by one of the other archdevils and had to send a representative to witness the event, he would stop it posthaste.

But he didn't.

So Gate suggested Rezzen take the tablet back and give it to the Openers. To open the Seal. Because he knew, Geryon didn't have the whole story.

Rezzen wasn't sure he could defeat the Closers, even with the Openers help. The Closers were stronger, and that's why the Seal had stayed closed for 5000 years.

So Gate gave him a pair of bracers. Not just any bracers.

You see, he enchanted those bracers with Wings herself. He put an Erinyes _inside_ the bracers. To learn. To fortify. And to kick ass.

And kicked ass they did.

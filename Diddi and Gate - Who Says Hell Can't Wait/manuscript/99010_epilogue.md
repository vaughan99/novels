# Epilogue

So that's how we ended up here.

What's that you say? You think your problem may be a little too hard? Need help?

Well, Quill's a good translator, knows several languages. And he is excellent at math. Don't have him create any contracts for you though... because, well, you know.

Mop has his slate of services. Off-the-books. I'd say he's a good bargain, if chaos is your thing.

Shoes? He's not a fighter. Don't go stealing him from me either. But he's good at looking like a snot-nosed kid asking stupid questions. He works for silver. Always dresses the same though. Not sure what he's saving up for.

If you're having street trouble, track down Gendarme. Depending on the problem, he may even go on field trips. His help, though, is very... quid pro quo. Expect to serve in a posse or two.

Now, Deadline is a master of medicine, but to get him to help you, you'll have to give interviews. Don't expect any anonymity either.

Kuthites are big fans of experiencing pain, but death is something they hate, because it cuts short the _experience_ of suffering. And the only way to tell your readers how to reproduce the same effects of what they're reading is to be a doctor. He can tell you the safest ways to experience the same thing.

He's also a fairly good illusionist and artist. He communicates with his editor by weaving illusions into his stories and sending them via their shared notebooks... ones that see what the other is writing. Nifty piece of magic.

Deadline likes to hang out at our business sometimes, trying to get a scoop on his next piece. 

Blood-Drop lives nearby too. He's always looking for people to impress. Hiring him shouldn't be too expensive... just make sure he keeps my blood jars out of his veins until he's ready to make things explode. And try to get his brother's wrinkle cream formula, will you?

Another one of my frequent flyers is also looking for work. Lets call her Aint Iomedae. She's a goblin, but don't let that put you off. She's from Isger, and she came upon a lady paladin dead. Attacked by goblins. Very sad.

Aint started looking through the paladin's belongings, and found her Iomedae's holy symbol, prayer book, banded mail, bastard sword, and pack pony. Warhorse was dead.

Aint can't read, but she flipped through the pictures in the prayer book, and found Iomedae fighting monsters and demons, finally landing her in heaven. Aint really wanted what she saw in that heaven, and swore to be a holy warrior of Iomedae.

She took the paladin's banded mail, bent it to her body, removed the extra bands, and broke the bastard sword to make it more... usable... to a goblin. She knew that knights had lances, but this one didn't. So she named the pack pony "Lance", and made it her warhorse.

The two set off in the direction the paladin was heading... Kaer Maga. Aint desperately wants to become the paladin, but she keeps running into that foul nature of hers. You can see her often outside Greenhalo's tent trying to get an audience, but really is super eager to join Gendarme's posses. For the experience.

She keeps coming to see me. Maybe I'm Golarion's cheapest paladin counselor. I'll bet she'll join you for free. Just understand she's a goblin. Who has zero fear. Let that sink in.

Rezzen? He's still around, and his Last Citadel Magrimite monks are useful for investigating around death. If you're bound for Ankar-Te or a vampire's den and you need someone to counteract those _negative vibes_, you could do worse than getting one of Rezzen's guys into your party.

Oh, and there's Wings. But you'd better have some serious magic or coin to trade. She's a Mammonite, honeydew. You have to make her help worth her time.

Looking for more work?

First of all, maybe you can look into what happened to Shoeless. With all our work lately, I almost totally forgot he went missing.

Now that Deadline is back to writing for _Sufferer's Digest_, he mentioned something about a girl who found wondering around the Flickering Tower before it disappeared again. It always appears and disappears, but this was the first time anybody had appeared out of nowhere around it.

She has no memory of who she is. She landed in the local orphanage... I can give you the address.

What got her on Deadline's beat was that she led a group of kids from the orphanage out late one night to explore one of the graveyards. When they got scared, she started... glowing. And what was shocking was that she also... was _shocking_. People that is. Well, and the horde of zombies from Ankar-Te that strolled by.

Then she started _vooping_. Well, not _vooping_, more like _zhipping_. She becomes a lightning bolt when other people get scared. Not all the time, mind you, just when things get truly frightening.

Deadline is planning an exclusive three-segment series on her. The loneliness of being a kid that scares others. The interviews with her _victims_ when she passed through them as a lightning bolt. And the pain of not knowing who she really is.

Finally, there's Gate. He has a number of needs. There are still a number of Geryon's secrets that are resisting becoming Mammon's secrets. Also, he is sometimes willing to offer some serious infernal muscle, but you'll learn pretty quick that Wings is cheap compared to him.

So now, give your name to Quill, and your occupation. Then tell me what you brought.

And don't worry, Geryon won't bite you. He just likes to... stare.

# Epilogue

So that's how we ended up here.

What's that you say? You think your problem may be a little too hard? Need help?

Well, Quill's a good translator, knows several languages. And he is excellent at math. And he's good at writing contracts... the sort that are fiendishly good.

Mop has his slate of services. Off-the-books. I'd say he's a good bargain, if chaos is your thing. Just make sure the butcher has good prices that day.

Shoes? He's not a fighter. Don't go stealing him from me either. But he's good at looking like a snot-nosed kid asking stupid questions. He works for silver. Always dresses the same though. Not sure what he's saving up for.

Remember the doppelganger's husband? I now refer folks to him for selling common iron and trinkets. Name's Cobblestone, and we're on his route. You'll see his cart when the sun's high. He doesn't stay long though... remember, he doesn't like Geryon staring at him.

If you're having street trouble, track down Gendarme. Depending on the problem, he may even go on field trips. His help, though, is very... how do infernals say? _Quid pro quo_? Expect to serve in a posse or two.

Now, Deadline is a master of medicine, but to get him to help you, you'll have to give interviews. Don't expect any anonymity either.

Kuthites are big fans of experiencing pain, but death is something they hate, because it cuts short the _experience_ of suffering. And the only way to tell your readers how to reproduce the same effects of what they're reading is to be a doctor. He can tell you the safest ways to experience the same thing.

He's also a fairly good illusionist, artist, and writer. He communicates with his editor by weaving illusions into his stories and sending them via their shared notebooks... ones that see what the other is writing. Nifty piece of magic.

Deadline likes to hang out at our business sometimes, trying to get a scoop on his next piece. 

Blood-Drop lives nearby too. He's always looking for people to impress. Hiring him shouldn't be too expensive... just make sure he keeps my blood jars out of his veins until he's ready to make things explode. And try to get his brother's wrinkle cream formula, will you?

Aint Iomedae keeps coming to see me. But not to _slay_ me. Maybe I'm Golarion's cheapest paladin counselor. You can see her often outside Greenhalo's tent trying to get an audience, but really is super eager to join Gendarme's posses. For the experience. I'll bet she'll join you for free. Just understand she's a goblin. Who has zero fear. Let that sink in. 

Rezzen? He's still around, and his Last Citadel monks are useful for investigating around death. If you're bound for Ankar-Te or a vampire's den and you need someone to counteract those _negative vibes_, you could do worse than getting one of Rezzen's guys into your party. I believe the one we call Urn is available for a contribution to the Last Citadel's coffers. Just ask any dwarf where the temple of Magrim is. And if Urn starts _staring at things_, let him.

Oh, and there's Wings. But you'd better have some serious magic or coin to trade. She's a Mammonite, honeydew. You have to make her help worth her time.

Forget hiring Gate as muscle, because he's too busy and you can't afford him. Unless you _can_. I'm sure he'll let you know.

Looking for more work?

First of all, maybe you can look into what happened to Shoeless. With all our work lately, I almost totally forgot he went missing.

Don't forget there's that cabal of doppelgangers out there. I'd help you track them down if you find more evidence. Talk to Cobblestone about where to start.

That key in Guard Dog's gullet? Still there. Made of mithral which I don't think is digestible. So, if you can coax it out of him, AND you're willing to travel all the way to Num-teria, then you might find something worth selling to Gate.

Gate has told me that extracting the key would be considered a _favor_ that he will be happy to repay too. Guard Dog apparently carried the key in his gullet into Gate's vault, which created quite the... interesting... accounting problem for Gate. Something about one asset eating another asset already in escrow. If Mammon's Unholy Auditors catch _that_, there would be _questions_. I'm sure Gate could claim ignorance, but I'm not sure that claim survives digestion.

Now that Deadline is back to writing for _Sufferer's Digest_, he always has items he needs me to focus on. And then he heads off to find the story. I'm sure he'd let you tag along so long as he gets your exclusive. Especially if you catch a gangrenous barbed arrow to the crotch.

Finally, there are still a number of Geryon's secrets that are resisting becoming Mammon's secrets. Gate can fill you in on those.

One final note: We're having a pot-luck dinner with the Church of the big "A" tomorrow. We're bringing Fezzark. The recipe was quite elaborate. And it truly does taste awful. I think it as a way to deliver poison at royal weddings when you hate the family of the bride or groom. Truly something that belonged in a hidden vault after all. I'm sure they'll love it.

Gate. Gate! Stop that infernal hammering, will you! You can build out your vault after they leave. And take down that ridiculous _Future Site of Bank of Mammon_ sign. We're not even finished moving in!

So now, give your name to Quill, and your occupation. Then tell me what you brought.

And don't worry, Geryon won't bite you. He just likes to... stare.

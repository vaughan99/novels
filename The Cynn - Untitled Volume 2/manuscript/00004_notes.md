Chapter Idea: Ketill is demanding the group train to fight.

He has seen multiple ways of fighting.

One-Eye absolutely hates training. Ketill says "Then no train. Circle."

"Circle?"

Ketill grabs One-Eye's bow and quiver, and mounts a horse. He rounds a tree, firing arrows into it from multiple directions.

"Circle. Like buzzard."

"That's still training."

"Training? Circling? Which more fun?"

"Neither."

"Circling keep you alive."

"Fine. Circling."



For Osric, he demands he un-learn shieldwall semantics. He demonstrates by charging Osric shield, then at the last moment stops and kicks Osric's shield, throwing him backward. Nobody was there behind him to keep him from falling.

"Why you on back?"

"Because you kicked me!"

"No. Why you on back?"

"You do that again and I'll punch you!"

"You still no learn. Shield wall not behind you. Where friends?"

"What?"

"Where Elswyth? Where Tor? Where One-Eye? Not behind you. That where. Elswyth by tree. Tor in wagon. One-Eye complaining, there."

Also for Osric: He tends to daze out, which can be fine in a center of a shieldwall, but not in small unit tactics.

"You eyes not right."

"What do you mean?"

"You stare."

"Ealhild said the same thing."

"You no battle stare. You stare stare. Dirt. Tree. Shield. Useless stare."

Ketill tapped on Osric's shield. "No stare. Look all. Quick. Spear. Eyes. Body. Open. Close. Strong not enough. Be fast."




Cynewulf is fine with training, until he gets nicked by Ketill. Then he loses it on Ketill, launching into his midsection and tackling him. Osric peels him off and holds him down.

"Get. Off. Me!"

"Not if you're trying to kill someone who isn't trying to kill you."

"I said..."

"When we trained with the fyrd shieldwall... We got to see every man's breaking point. Everyone's temper was tested."

"Didn't you hear him, you _idiot_? This is NOT a shieldwall!"

Cynewulf started reaching towards the shank in his armwrap.

Osric caught his arm and pressed it into the dirt.

"You’re right. A shieldwall has men who trust each other to keep them alive... We're not a shieldwall. But we better start behaving like one, even if we don't fight like one. So, hello, my name is Osric. And I’m here to make sure you don’t die. Nice to meet you. This is Ketill."



Tor is 45 with bad knees. Ketill says: "Fine. Enemy have bad knee. You fight him."



Murdock is a 32-year-old wagon master who relies on 2 crossbows and is fairly good in a fight with a hand axe and targe. He never trained in a shieldwall, only in a wagon-fort with small unit tactics.

Ketill sparred with him for a minute. In the middle of the sparring, Ketill tried everything. Stomping his foot. Aiming a blow to the knee. Feinting right with a blow to the left. Nothing worked.

"You good. Feet good. Eyes good. Shield good."

"That's it?!"

"More training? Let enemy give scars."


For Elswyth... he teaches her to hunt, not fight.

"You hunt. No fight."

"Hunt? What do you mean?"

"Strike when nobody look. Hide when look."

"Shouldn't I get a shield. One-Eye and I are the only ones without one."

"No."

"No shield?"

"No."

"Should I fight with my blade? Stab them from behind?"

"No. You chop down tree with blade?"

"Well, no."

"Good. Chop down enemy with axe. Then hide with tree."

"Where we fight, there might not be any trees."

He pointed to Osric.

"Tree. And keep tree up."



Anecdote for why training is important:

“Magyar bring me battle-skin. Taken in raid. Broken here, wide here.”

Ketill touched his ribs and shoulder.

“I repair. Make fit. Magyar go train."

“And it failed?”

“In practice. Strap tear when he turn and draw. Arrow loose. Hit master in butt.”

“You made poor armor?”

“No. I young. Made young battle-skin.”

"How did the master take it?"

"Don't know. Didn't stay. I stay? Two arrows my butt."
